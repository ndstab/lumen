## Continuous version

For reading straight through without watching the table.

> This is Lumen. Three science courses for Classes 8 and 9, and this learner has
> already made a start on two of them.
>
> Inside a course, every lesson carries its own state.
>
> A lesson is three things at once. A video, a reading, and a quiz. The figures
> are drawn to correct geometry. Two construction rays, meeting exactly where
> the image really forms.
>
> The player is written for this app rather than embedded. That is the decision
> everything else rests on. Every play, pause and seek is recorded with the
> position it happened at. Speed changes too.
>
> Then the quiz. Six questions, unlimited retakes. Grading happens on the
> server, so the answers are never sitting in the page. A wrong answer gets the
> explanation, not just a cross.
>
> Now the teacher's view. Sixteen hundred events recorded. Seventy-two lessons
> opened, eighty-seven quizzes submitted. Clicks, page views, quiz answers. Each
> one a sentence naming who did what.
>
> Filtered to video: three hundred and nineteen events. Paused. Rate changed.
> Buffering. Seeked. Started.
>
> The analytics. Of sixty-six video starts, ninety-seven percent got past the
> first quarter, sixty-one percent reached half, and eight percent reached the
> end. That cliff is where the explanation is losing people.
>
> Which questions are actually hard, across every attempt including retakes.
>
> And when the work actually happens.

## Notes for recording

- **The two long stretches are 0:22 to 0:30 and 0:45 to 0:54.** Those are where
  to slow down. Everywhere else the picture changes every three seconds or so.
- **There is no cursor in the video**, so avoid saying "here I click". Describe
  what appears instead, which is what the lines above do.
- **The educator view arrives at 0:45 with no visible sign-in.** "Now the
  teacher's view" covers the jump without drawing attention to it.
- **If you run long**, the two lines that can go without losing anything are
  "Speed changes too" at 0:30 and "Inside a course, every lesson carries its own
  state" at 0:09.
- **If you run short**, add after the drop-off line at 1:15: "None of which is
  visible from a grade book."
